Project Overview

Strategic Location

Naresho, Kajiado County lies within one of Kenya's highest solar irradiation zones — delivering strong, consistent solar resource profiles that support competitive energy yield assumptions and long-term generation stability.

Exceptional Grid Access

The Project is in immediate proximity to a 400 kV KETRACO transmission substation forming part of Kenya's national grid. This substation serves major grid-connected assets including the Kipeto Wind Farm (majority shareholder: Meridiam) — placing Narresho within an established institutional-grade renewable energy corridor with direct access to high-voltage transmission infrastructure.

Land & Tenure

The acting project sponsor, Mr. Anthony Timayio, together with five (5) other entrepreneurs, collectively own and control approximately 800 acres of land required for development. Landowners will grant long-term lease rights to a dedicated HoldCo, which will in turn sub-lease to the Project SPV — providing clean land governance and bankable contractual arrangements.

Corporate Governance

A dedicated HoldCo is being incorporated as the Project Sponsor to institutionalise the structure and ensure bankability. The HoldCo will hold all project assets and contractual arrangements, with clear governance and an investor-ready framework suitable for capital participation at scale.

Development Path

Development has commenced with legal, technical and financial pre-feasibility to validate land rights, confirm achievable capacity, assess interconnection configuration and establish preliminary project cost. Subject to pre-feasibility confirmation, the Project will advance into full feasibility studies including ESIA, grid interconnection study, detailed financial modelling and PPA negotiation.
